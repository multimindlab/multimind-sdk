## 🧠 What is MultiMind SDK?

MultiMind SDK is a **universal orchestration and fine-tuning framework** for both transformer and non-transformer models. It is designed to help developers, researchers, and AI engineers:

- Build RAG pipelines
- Create and evaluate agents
- Fine-tune models like GPT, QLoRA, RWKV, Mamba
- Route and serve LLMs at scale

## 🎯 Why Index MultiMind SDK?

This SDK is:
- Lightweight
- Model-agnostic
- A viable alternative to LangChain or LlamaIndex
- Production-ready with routing, training, and evaluation built-in
